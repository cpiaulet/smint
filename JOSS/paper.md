# Summary

'SMINT' is a planet structure model interpolator that obtains posterior distributions of the core, H2O and H/He mass fractions for a given planet. 'SMINT" interpolates unto the Lopex & Fortney (2014), Zeng (2016), Aguichine et al. (2021), and now Aguichine et al. (2025) and Tang et al. (2025) structure model grids. Through Bayesian fitting, 'SMINT' can then conduct a retrieval to obtain posterior probability distributions of the input planet's structure under one of the given composition assumptions. Outputs of this tool are corner plots of the posterior probability distributions for each planet parameter and a mass-radius curve. For Water World composition inference across the Aguichine et al. (2025) model grids, the user can look at a back-tracing panel to see the input planets inferred thermal evolution.
